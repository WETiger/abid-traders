About Us

Abid Traders Is A Private Company Managed By The Professionals.

Abid Traders Was Established In 2008 As An Engineering Contracting Firm With A Vision To Provide Quality Product Supply And Services In The fieldsof Renewable Energy, Solar, Surveillance, Civil, Electrical, Telecommunication And Mechanical Works In Bangladesh.

Abid Traders Is Managed By Highly Qualified, Experienced, And Dedicated Professionals havespecialization In Their Respective Field Covering A Wide Spectrum Of Engineering Disciplines Including Design, Production, Monitoring, Evaluation, Consultancy Etc.

Abid Traders Having Highly Competent Technical Team With Vast Experience Of Various Projects Today Became As One Of The Most Reliable Company In The Field Of Electrical, Mechanical And Civil Works In Bangladesh.

Abid Traders Import All typesof Spear Parts For Power Generation & Distribution Items As Well As All typesof Refrigerant Gas Along With Various Chemicals. In The Last 14 Years, Abid Traders Has Successfully Completed a Large Number Of Sub-Station & Power Generation Related Various Supply, Installation, Testing, And Commissioning Countrywide.

Abid Traders Having The G-2-G“Power grid network strengthening project under” The PGCB (Power Grid Company of Bangladesh).

Recently, a Recruiting License from the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training (BMET) Named Sajid Overseas Ltd.
